ABOUT CLARICARE

Clarity in Skincare.
Care Beyond the Surface.

ClariCare is a botanical skincare and cosmetic formulation company that brings together nature, cosmetic science, and innovation to create skincare products and services.

We develop and sell skincare products inspired by botanical ingredients and guided by modern cosmetic formulation principles. Alongside our products, we provide formulation services and learning opportunities for people and emerging brands.

Our approach is built on thoughtful formulation, ingredient transparency, quality, and responsible care for people and the environment.

Delivery & Returns Policy

        
Last updated: 25 October 2026

        
This policy explains how ClariCare Botanica Ltd delivers orders and handles eligible return or exchange requests. It does not limit any rights available under applicable Kenyan law.

        
Delivery charges and timing

        
    
          
  • Nairobi: KSh 700; usually 1–3 hours on business days.
    • 
          
  • Mombasa: KSh 2,500; usually the next business day.
    • 
          
  • Other counties: KSh 1,500; typically 1–3 working days in major towns and 2–5 working days elsewhere.
    • 
          
  • Orders above KSh 5,000 qualify for free delivery.
    • 
        

        
Processing and tracking

        
Orders placed Monday to Friday are processed during business hours. Weekend or public-holiday orders received before noon East Africa Time may be processed the same day; later orders are handled on the next business day. We aim to confirm orders within 30 minutes and share dispatch or tracking details by WhatsApp or email when the courier collects them. Delivery estimates can vary by location and courier availability.

        
If no one is available to receive a delivery, the courier may try again on the next business day. After two unsuccessful attempts, the order may be held for up to 48 hours before being cancelled and returned to stock.

        
Returns and exchanges

        
Contact us within 24 hours after delivery if an item is damaged, defective, materially different from its description, or incorrect. Unused and unopened products in their original, resaleable packaging may also be considered for an exchange or store credit. Hygiene-sensitive items must remain sealed. Items marked final sale, gift cards, store credit, and opened or used products are not eligible unless required by law or the item is defective. Eligible returns are normally resolved with an exchange or store credit rather than a cash refund, except where a refund or other remedy is required by law.

        
To request help, provide your order number, the item, and the reason by WhatsApp or email. We aim to respond within one business day. If approved, Nairobi collection is arranged at no charge; for other areas we will agree on a courier drop-off or collection. We cover return courier costs for defective or incorrect items. Approved exchanges or store credits are generally processed within three business days after the returned item has been received and checked.

        
Order cancellation and damaged deliveries

        
Contact us as soon as possible to cancel an order that has not been dispatched; an eligible refund for a cancelled, undispatched order is processed to the original payment method within 3–5 business days. Once dispatched, an order cannot normally be cancelled, but you may request a return under this policy. If a parcel appears damaged, photograph the parcel and product before use and contact us within 24 hours. Any statutory cancellation or consumer rights that apply remain unaffected.
